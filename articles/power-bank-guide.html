How to Choose a Power Bank

Capacity, output, and charging speed explained without the jargon.

Capacity (mAh)

This determines how many phone charges you'll get. As a rough guide, a phone battery is typically 3,000–5,000 mAh, so a 10,000 mAh power bank gives roughly two full charges, accounting for efficiency losses.

Output and input

Check the wattage output matches what your device can accept — a higher-wattage power bank charges compatible fast-charging phones and laptops noticeably quicker.

Ports

USB-C has become the most common standard across phones and laptops; a power bank with at least one USB-C port future-proofs it better than USB-A only.

Size vs capacity trade-off

Larger capacity means more weight and bulk. For daily carry, a mid-size (around 10,000 mAh) bank is usually the practical sweet spot; for travel, a larger one may be worth the extra weight.
