Best Accessories for a Productive Home Office

The small upgrades that make the biggest difference to comfort and focus.

Start with what touches your body

Chairs, keyboards, and monitor height affect comfort every single day, which is why they're usually the highest-value upgrades — more so than a flashier laptop.

Monitor height and an external screen

Raising a laptop screen to eye level (even with a stand or a stack of books) reduces neck strain. A single external monitor, even a modest one, is one of the most commonly recommended productivity upgrades for home workers.

Lighting

A desk lamp with adjustable brightness reduces eye strain during video calls and evening work, and matters more than most people expect until they try it.

Cable and desk organization

Reducing visual clutter on a desk is a small change that consistently gets reported as improving focus — it costs little and takes an afternoon to set up.
