What to Check Before Buying Headphones

Fit, noise isolation, and battery life — the checklist before you check out.

Fit and comfort

Over-ear, on-ear, and in-ear designs feel very different for long sessions. If you'll wear them for hours (lectures, calls, commuting), comfort over time matters more than any spec.

Noise cancellation vs noise isolation

Active noise cancellation (ANC) electronically reduces ambient sound and is useful for travel or noisy environments. Passive noise isolation (a snug physical fit) helps too, and doesn't drain a battery.

Battery life

For wireless headphones, look at both single-charge battery life and total life with the charging case (for earbuds) — the marketed number is usually the single-charge figure.

Wired vs wireless

Wired headphones avoid battery and pairing issues entirely and are often cheaper for the same sound quality, but wireless offers more freedom of movement — the right choice depends on how you'll actually use them.
