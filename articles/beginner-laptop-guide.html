Laptop Buying Guide for Beginners

New to buying tech? Start here before you spend a cedi or a dollar.

Decide your budget range first

Set a realistic range before you start browsing — it narrows choices fast and stops spec-chasing. Leave a little room for accessories (a case, a charger backup).

Windows, macOS, or ChromeOS?

Windows laptops offer the widest range of prices and hardware. macOS (Apple) tends to cost more but has strong battery life and build quality. ChromeOS devices are simple, affordable, and well suited to browser-based work, but less flexible for installed software.

New vs refurbished

A reputable refurbished laptop from an official or well-reviewed seller can be excellent value, provided it comes with a warranty or return window.

Common beginner mistakes

  • Buying based on brand name alone rather than the actual configuration (RAM/storage) inside.
  • Ignoring the keyboard and trackpad feel — you'll use these every day.
  • Skipping the return policy check.
