How Much RAM Do You Really Need?

A plain-English breakdown of 8GB vs 16GB vs 32GB, and who each option actually suits.

What RAM actually does

RAM is short-term working memory — it's what lets your device keep several apps and browser tabs open at once without slowing down. More RAM doesn't make individual tasks faster; it lets you juggle more things simultaneously.

8GB

Fine for browsing, documents, email, and streaming. It can feel tight if you regularly keep 20+ browser tabs open alongside other apps.

16GB

A comfortable middle ground for students and remote workers — handles heavy multitasking, light photo editing, and most everyday software without strain.

32GB or more

Really only worth paying for if you do video editing, run virtual machines, or work with large datasets. Most everyday users won't notice a difference over 16GB.

Rule of thumb: buy for how you multitask, not for a big number on a spec sheet.
