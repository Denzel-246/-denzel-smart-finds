Essential Tech Accessories for University Students

What's genuinely useful in lecture halls and dorm rooms — and what's a waste of money.

Genuinely useful

  • A reliable backpack with a padded laptop sleeve — protects your most expensive purchase.
  • A compact power bank — for long days on campus away from outlets.
  • A pair of comfortable headphones — for libraries, shared rooms, and online lectures.
  • A basic external hard drive or cloud backup — losing coursework to a dead drive is avoidable.

Often unnecessary

Specialty "student edition" gadgets and heavily bundled accessory kits are frequently more about marketing than genuine need — check whether you'd buy each item on its own before buying the bundle.
