Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Denzel Smart Finds ("we", "us", "this site") handles information when you visit denzelsmartfinds.com.

Information we collect

We do not require account creation to read this site. We may collect limited information in the following cases:

  • Contact form: if you submit the contact form, we receive the name, email, and message you provide.
  • Newsletter signup: if you subscribe, your email address is collected for the purpose of sending updates. (This site's newsletter form is not yet connected to an email provider — see disclosure below.)
  • Basic analytics: like most websites, standard hosting and analytics services may automatically log information such as browser type, approximate location, and pages visited.

Cookies

This site may use basic cookies for functionality and analytics purposes. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

Affiliate links and third-party sites

This site contains affiliate links, including Amazon Associates links. When you click these links and make a purchase, the third-party retailer (such as Amazon) may collect information under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit.

How information is used

Any information collected is used only to respond to inquiries, operate the newsletter (once connected to a provider), and understand site usage to improve content.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers strictly necessary to operate the site (for example, a future email newsletter provider or hosting service).

Your choices

You may unsubscribe from any newsletter at any time, and you can contact us to ask what information we hold about you.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@denzelsmartfinds.com.
