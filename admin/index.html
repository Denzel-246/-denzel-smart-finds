Editing guide

This is a static website — there's no database and no login. Everything you'll want to change day-to-day lives in one file: js/data.js. This page explains what to edit and where.

1 Edit site content

Open js/data.js in any text editor (Notepad, VS Code, or even a plain text app). It contains four sections:

  • SITE — your site name, tagline, contact email, and social links.
  • CATEGORIES — the 6 category tiles and nav menu.
  • ARTICLES — every guide/article card, and which page it links to.
  • PRODUCTS — every product card, including price, rating, image, and Amazon link placeholders.

To add a new product or article: copy an existing entry (the whole { ... } block), paste it, and change the text inside the quotes. Save the file and refresh your browser — no build step required.

2 Add your Amazon Associates link

Once approved for Amazon Associates, find your tracking ID (it looks like yoursite-20). In js/data.js, replace every:

"amazonUrl": "AMAZON_LINK_PLACEHOLDER"

with your real product link plus your tag, for example:

"amazonUrl": "https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0EXAMPLE?tag=yoursite-20"

Also replace matching PRICE_PLACEHOLDER, PRODUCT_IMAGE_PLACEHOLDER, and RATING_PLACEHOLDER values only once you've verified them directly on Amazon — never guess a price.

3 Replace product images

Save your image files into the /images folder, then in js/data.js change:

"image": "PRODUCT_IMAGE_PLACEHOLDER"

to a path like "image": "images/laptop-1.jpg". (The current placeholder text keeps the site looking correct with no images yet — it won't break anything if you leave it as-is for now.)

4 Connect the contact and newsletter forms

Both forms currently only show a message on submit — they don't send email yet, because that requires a backend. Easiest beginner options:

  • Contact form: sign up for a free plan with a form service like Formspree or Getform, then point the form's action attribute (in contact.html) at the URL they give you.
  • Newsletter: sign up with Mailchimp, Brevo, or similar, and paste the embed form action URL into the newsletter form's action attribute (in index.html) and update SITE.newsletterProvider in js/data.js as a reminder of what you connected.

5 Update your domain everywhere

Once you buy denzelsmartfinds.com (or another domain), update:

  • SITE.domain in js/data.js
  • The canonical URL and Open Graph tags at the top of every page (search for denzelsmartfinds.com)
  • sitemap.xml and robots.txt
